Project Overview & Architecture
NodeSense: Explainable and Robust GNNs on Citation Networks

Scientific literature contains dense citation structures where papers learn not only from their own abstract keywords, but from neighborhood citation context. NodeSense benchmarks a feature-only MLP Baseline against GCN (spectral Laplacian averaging), GAT (multi-head attention), and GraphSAGE (inductive neighborhood pooling).

Empirical Finding: As citation dropout increases to 50%, GraphSAGE exhibits the highest resilience with only a 1.40% accuracy drop, while GCN drops by 2.27% and GAT by 2.17%. The feature-only MLP remains completely invariant (73.30%) as it does not rely on topological edges.
